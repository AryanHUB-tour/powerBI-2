## **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** 



Dataset Overview

There are total 1001 books. The dataset contains book-level information with one numeric variable (price) and two categorical variables (title and availability).





**Key Questions**



* Are most books in stock or out of stock?



* Are there any unusually expensive books?





**Trends and Patterns**



* Most books fall within a moderate price range.



* The majority of books are currently in stock.





**Anomalies and Data Issues**



* A few books appear priced significantly higher than others.



* Prices originally contained currency symbols and were cleaned.



* The dataset is limited to one website and one category.



* Few books appear priced significantly higher than the rest, which may be considered outliers.







**Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Summary**



* The dataset contains book-level data including title, price, and availability.



* Price is the only numeric variable, while title and availability are categorical.



* Most books fall within a moderate price range, with a few higher-priced outliers.



* The majority of books are currently in stock.



* No strong relationship was observed between book price and availability.



* Some data quality issues were identified, such as currency symbols in price values, which were resolved during data cleaning.



* The dataset is limited to a single website and category, which may affect general conclusions.
